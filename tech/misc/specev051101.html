 Pulse X-band spectrometer ( known as X-band-2 ).  This spectrometer was renovated  recently after many years of silence.  Main reason for that was to have an instrument where new pulse programmer could be implemented and tested without interrupting of  others research projects . This goal is still valid, but hopefully it will be reached this year.  So, we can go little bit further and think about  f. e. really new generation of the ETH pulse spectrometer which will match current modern requirements  and future challenge ( no body knows future but ... you can guess). Any way it should be kind of ETH answer  (quiet) to Bruker  "New Technology Bridge".  In an other word, X-band-2 could be a play ground for next 2 years not only for measuring system only (new pulse programmer-> software->fast digitizer) but also for alternative main and auxiliary mw sources (PLO, synthesizer  etc.), pulse forming unit design ( phase manipulators and remote controlled phase shifters,  bichromatic pulses ...).   Beside this it is nice to have spectrometer band width which  covers all our X-band probes: commercial Varian/Bruker  and home-built.  Back

Pulse S-band spectrometer.  Here CW detection could be  installed (Jeff's idea).  We can buy S-band tail for Bruker FlexLine  probe head ( rest we have), then we need  modulation amplifier =< 100 kHz ( f.e. from old Bruker console) and we can use Bruker's Signal Channel modules ( we have several ER-023) for demodulation. Back

Pulse Q-band spectrometer.   Several problems still exist  here.  Two most important are :
1) CW detection;  2) Weak sensitivity in pulse mode with ER-5106QTE probe (Bruker, 1.6 mm sample tube) ;
  To solve  a first problem need to do  almost everything what needed for CW:  i.e.  replace ( or repair )  modulation modules ( oscillator-amplifier-PSD-ADC) and microwave bridge ( input circuits-circulator and AFC system). Modulation stuff we will buy from Bruker ( the same set as for E500, or E580) what to do with the mw part - no idea, but the biggest problem here.  Second problem- weak sensitivity with ER-5106 QTE, could ( and should ) be solved in two ways
simultaneously:  improve the receiver ( more gain at mw, tunable bandwidth for video amplifier,  nicely matched line
preamplifier - scale amplifier to eliminate a ringing, etc.).   Second  part is concern to ER-5106 probe itself. I do believe that we can increase sample size up to 2 mm, at least the hole in the cavity allows to do this. The problem(s)  are to make sample holder/ ruler which is able to  keep  sample tube position  exactly coaxial with cavity axis;  then it will be necessary to play little bit with ENDOR coil inside. And finally, in case of success, to sell probe back to Bruker for the double price.   Back
