Microwave studio

This is a tool for high-frequency electromagnetic field computations. MICROWAVE STUDIO (CST MWS) has shape modeler, including adaptive mesh generator, and powerful time domain solver , as well as many useful utilities to deal with 3D field, S-parameters etc. 3.10.03
