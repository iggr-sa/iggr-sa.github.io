Welcome to Igor Gromov web page!

Hello! I am Igor Gromov - experimental radio physicist working in the field of Electron Paramagnetic Resonance (EPR) spectroscopy. My interest covers EPR methodology, its application in biology, chemistry and physics; computational EPR and instrumentation. This web page is an overview of my current activity and completed projects.
